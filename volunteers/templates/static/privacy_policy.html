Privacy Policy

FOSDEM Volunteers collects and stores personal data that you provide during registration in order to coordinate volunteers for the FOSDEM conference. This data is used solely for the purpose of volunteer management.

Data collected

  • Name and username
  • Email address
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • Contact details provided voluntarily (mobile number, Matrix ID)

Your rights

You may request deletion of your data by contacting the volunteer coordinators.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the volunteer coordinators.
