Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 (version 2)

FOSDEM Volunteers collects and stores personal data that you provide during registration in order to coordinate volunteers for the FOSDEM conference. This data is used solely for the purpose of volunteer management.

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller for this website is FOSDEM VZW (Belgian enterprise number / KBO-BCE: BE 0477.085.590), a not-for-profit organisation under Belgian law, registered at Guldendelle 9, 1930 Zaventem, Belgium. For any privacy-related question or request you can contact us at {{ "info@fosdem.org"|obfuscate_email }} or {{ "volunteer-admin@fosdem.org"|obfuscate_email }}. FOSDEM VZW is a small volunteer-run non-profit and is not required to appoint a Data Protection Officer under Article 37 GDPR; the contacts above act as our privacy contact point.

Legal basis for processing

We rely on different legal bases depending on the data and purpose:

  • Necessity of the volunteer relationship (Art. 6(1)(b)/(f) GDPR) — your name, username, email address, and task sign-up/attendance records. These are required to create a volunteer account and to schedule and coordinate you for tasks; without them we cannot register you as a volunteer.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) — all optional fields (profile photo, mobile number, Matrix ID, pronouns, spoken languages, T-shirt size). These are never required to volunteer; you can leave them blank, add them, or remove them at any time from your profile, and doing so has no effect on your ability to sign up for or carry out tasks.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — printing badges/labels and retaining volunteering history across editions, in our legitimate interest of running the FOSDEM volunteer programme safely and effectively. We have assessed that this is necessary and proportionate: it uses the minimum data needed, access to history/attendance data is restricted to you and coordinators/admins (see "Who can see your data" below), and you may object at any time (see "Your rights").

You can withdraw consent for any consent-based (optional) data at any time by editing your profile or by contacting us; this does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal. Withdrawing consent for account-necessary data (e.g. deleting your account) means we can no longer keep you registered as a volunteer.

Data collected

Fields marked "(optional)" are never required to volunteer and can be left blank.

  • Name and username — required to create your account and identify you for tasks.
  • Email address — required, used to log in and to send you account and task-related emails.
  • Profile photo (optional) — shown on your profile to help coordinators and fellow volunteers recognise you.
  • "About me" text (optional) — a short bio shown on your profile.
  • Contact details
    • Mobile number (optional) — used as an on-site emergency/urgent contact channel.
    • Matrix ID (optional) — used to invite you to volunteer chat rooms.
  • Pronouns (optional) — shown on your profile and badge so others can address you correctly.
  • Spoken languages (optional) — shown on your badge so attendees know which languages you can help in.
  • T-shirt size (optional) — used to help gauge the number of T-shirts of each size to order; providing this does not guarantee you will receive a T-shirt in that size. Visible only to you and volunteer coordinators/admins.
  • Task sign-up and attendance records, including sign-in/sign-out timestamps for tasks you check in to — required for scheduling and coordination.

How your data is used

  • To schedule and coordinate your volunteering tasks for each edition.
  • Your name, username, pronouns, and spoken languages may be printed on a physical badge/lanyard label for the event, so staff, other volunteers, and attendees can recognise you and communicate with you appropriately during FOSDEM. Your task schedule for the current edition may also be printed on the label, to help you as a volunteer know where you should be and when. This label is printed by FOSDEM itself, on-site, and is never sent to or printed by an external/third-party provider.
  • Task sign-in/sign-out timestamps help coordinators spot tasks that are unstaffed or running short, so they can quickly find and dispatch a replacement volunteer to cover the gap.

Who can see your data

  • You can always see your own full profile, task history, and current-edition tasks.
  • Volunteer coordinators and site admins can see your full profile, including your volunteering history, current-edition tasks, and T-shirt size, in order to schedule and support you.
  • Other logged-in volunteers can see your name, username, profile photo, "about me" text, and pronouns, but not your contact details, T-shirt size, volunteering history, or current-edition tasks.
  • Staff, other volunteers, and attendees may see whatever is printed on your physical badge/lanyard label (name, pronouns, spoken languages, task schedule — see above); printed labels are discarded after the event and are not retained by FOSDEM.

Data retention and volunteering history

Your task assignments are retained across editions rather than deleted after each event, so that your profile shows a "Volunteering history" of tasks from previous years. This history, as well as your task list for the current edition, is only visible to you and to volunteer coordinators/admins — it is not visible to other volunteers. This history is kept for as long as your account remains active, as it directly supports showing you your own volunteering history and helping coordinators recognise returning volunteers. If your account is deleted, your task and attendance records are deleted or anonymised within 90 days. You can also request deletion of your history at any time — see "Your rights" below. Optional profile fields (photo, contact details, pronouns, languages, T-shirt size) are kept for as long as you choose to keep them on your profile, and are removed if you delete them or your account. Routine backups are retained for a limited period (no more than 30 days) before being overwritten.

Cookies

This website only uses strictly necessary cookies, which are exempt from consent requirements under Belgian ePrivacy rules because they are necessary to provide the service you request:

  • Session cookie — keeps you signed in; expires when your session ends or after a period of inactivity.
  • CSRF cookie — protects forms against cross-site request forgery; expires after about one year.

We do not use tracking, advertising, or analytics cookies.

Automated decision-making

We do not use automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects on you.

Minors

You must be at least 16 years old to volunteer for FOSDEM. Some tasks may, at the discretion of volunteer coordinators or other staff, be restricted to volunteers aged 18 and over. When signing in for the first time, you may be asked to prove your age to a member of staff.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse, including access restrictions, encrypted connections (HTTPS), and restricting historical/attendance data to yourself and volunteer coordinators/admins. In the unlikely event of a data breach affecting your data, we will notify the Belgian Data Protection Authority and, where required, you, in line with Articles 33 and 34 GDPR.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, export (data portability), or request deletion of your data (including your volunteering history), and to request restriction of its processing. Where we rely on legitimate interest (see "Legal basis for processing"), you also have the right to object at any time to that processing; we will then stop unless we have compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests. We will respond to any such request without undue delay, and in any event within one month (extendable by a further two months for complex requests). To exercise any of these rights, contact us at {{ "info@fosdem.org"|obfuscate_email }} or {{ "volunteer-admin@fosdem.org"|obfuscate_email }}. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données), Rue de la Presse 35 / Drukpersstraat 35, 1000 Brussels, Belgium, at autoriteprotectiondonnees.be (opens in new tab), or with the supervisory authority of your own country of residence, without prejudice to your right to a judicial remedy.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will ask existing users to review and accept the updated policy the next time they sign in, and we will update the "version" and "last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to {{ "info@fosdem.org"|obfuscate_email }} or {{ "volunteer-admin@fosdem.org"|obfuscate_email }}.
