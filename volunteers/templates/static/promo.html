FOSDEM

FOSDEM is a free and non-commercial two-day event organised by volunteers to promote the widespread use of free and open source software.

Documentation

Task documentation for Volunteers can be found on doc.volunteers.fosdem.org.

Volunteering

FOSDEM needs you! Every year, the FOSDEM team is assisted by an enthusiastic team of volunteers to help out with various tasks and make the event a fun and safe place for all visitors.

Location

FOSDEM 2026 will take place at the ULB in Brussels, Belgium, within many of the buildings on the Solbosch campus.
